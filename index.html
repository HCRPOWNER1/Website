Server Rules

Follow these rules to keep TSRP professional and enjoyable.

01 — Respect Everyone

Treat all members, staff and players respectfully.

02 — No RDM

Randomly attacking or killing other players is prohibited.

03 — No VDM

Do not intentionally use vehicles to attack players.

04 — Follow Staff Instructions

Staff directions must be followed during sessions.

05 — Stay In Character

Keep roleplay realistic and appropriate during sessions.

06 — No Exploiting

Exploiting, abusing glitches or cheating is prohibited.
